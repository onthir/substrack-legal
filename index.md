# SubsTrack — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 May 2026
**Developer:** onthir
**Contact:** bibek09@outlook.com

SubsTrack is designed with one principle: your data stays on your device. This policy explains what that means in detail.

## Data we collect

**None.** SubsTrack does not collect, transmit, or receive any personal data from your device. There are no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.

The data you create inside the app — substances you've added, log entries, notes, timestamps — is stored locally in a private SQLite database on your device. We have no access to it.

## Data we share

**None.** Because we collect no data, there is nothing to share with third parties, advertisers, or governments. There is no backend service that could be subpoenaed or breached.

## Permissions

SubsTrack requests no special device permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, or any other system resource.

## Third-party services

SubsTrack does not integrate with any third-party services for analytics, ads, or cloud storage. The only external network calls made by the app are to:

- Helpline / support resource websites when you tap them on the Support screen (e.g. findahelpline.com, samhsa.gov). These are user-initiated; we do not communicate with these services on your behalf.

## Data retention

Your data exists on your device until you choose to delete it. You can clear individual entries from inside the app, or wipe everything by clearing the app's storage in your phone's system settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Backups

On Android, your device may include SubsTrack's database in its automatic Google Drive backup if you have system-wide backup enabled. This is controlled by Android itself, not by SubsTrack. To opt out, disable backup for SubsTrack in Settings → System → Backup.

On iOS, the database is included in standard iCloud backups if enabled. To opt out, exclude SubsTrack in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.

## Children's privacy

SubsTrack is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — including children — because we do not collect data at all.

## Your rights

Because all data stays local to your device, you have complete control. You can view, edit, or delete any data at any time directly within the app. No request, ticket, or correspondence is needed.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, the new policy will be published at this same URL with an updated effective date.
